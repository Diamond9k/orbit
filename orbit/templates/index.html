How it works

Three steps, nothing to install

1
Name a model

Type a Hugging Face model name. Orbit reads its size and tells you which machines it fits on. If it can't tell, it says so.

2
Add hardware time

Pay by card for time on a machine. Shown in dollars and minutes. Not tokens, not credits.

3
Start

The machine is yours alone. The model loads. You talk to it, and a guide answers your questions along the way.

What you pay

One number. Per hour. Set by the host.

You see the price before you start. You are charged by the minute while the session runs. When you stop, you see what you used and what is left. Nothing else is ever charged.

What Orbit keeps

A list and a ledger.

Orbit knows which machines are online and what you paid. Your model and your conversation run on the host and go straight to your browser. Orbit's server is built so it cannot read them.

Have an idle GPU?

List it. You are paid for every hour someone uses it, and nothing when they don't. Works from a normal gaming PC behind a home router.
