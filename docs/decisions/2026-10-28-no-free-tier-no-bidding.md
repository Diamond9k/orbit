# Nothing is free. No bidding. One user, one machine.

Date: 2026-10-28

There is no free tier. Hardware time is the only way in and the guide exists only inside a
paid session. Giving things away lowers the value of the paid thing.

Sessions are one user on one machine, exclusive, at a fixed price per hour set by the host.
No interruptible tier and no bidding: a session is never paused because someone outbid you.

Hosts are paid only when their machine is used, never for listing it.

Why: these are what make Orbit different from a datacenter and from GPU marketplaces built
for engineers. They are also what keep the money flow simple and honest.
